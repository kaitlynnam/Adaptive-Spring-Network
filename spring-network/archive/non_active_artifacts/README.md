# Non-active generated artifacts

These tables and datasets do not belong to the active profile-conditioned
passive study on `internal_fan_3d_60_spring.json`.

- `tables/isaaclab/` and `data/isaaclab/` — robot rollout work.
- `tables/legacy/` — historical experiments.
- `tables/profile_conditioned_passive/` — planar passive experiments.
- `tables/profile_conditioned_passive_3d_non_active/` — alternate 24-, 30-,
  and 48-spring oracle results plus short linear/nonlinear screening runs.
- `models/isaaclab/` and `isaaclab/` — all IsaacLab checkpoints and source.
- `models/legacy/` — all historical and `adaptive_trained_*` checkpoints.
- `models/profile_conditioned_passive/` — planar passive checkpoints.
- `models/profile_conditioned_passive_3d_screens/` — non-final linear and
  nonlinear 3D screens.
- `models/superseded_60spring_variants/` and matching tables/plots — initial
  zero-refresh, rerelaxed two-refresh, and four-refresh naming experiments.
- `topologies/` — preload, planar, legacy, alternate spatial, and randomized
  topology-search definitions.
- `source/` — planar visualization and topology-search/generation utilities.

They are preserved for traceability but must not be used in current paper
tables or aggregate plots.
